Legal help in the language people actually speak

From a spoken problem to a ready-to-file complaint — in your own language.

Most people in India never reach a lawyer for their first question. LegalListen does what a good lawyer does in a first meeting: it listens to the story in Kannada, English, Hindi, Kanglish or Hinglish, asks only the questions that matter, separates what is proved from what is only said, finds the exact law from the official text, and writes the first document the person needs.

The pipeline

  1. 🎙ListenTyping or speech (Gemini speech-to-text or the browser). Code-mixed input like “nanna anna fake documents maadidaane” is normal input, not an error.
  2. 🧠UnderstandLocal NLU (TF-IDF char + word n-grams → logistic regression) plus rules for dates, people, money, negation and danger; an LLM refines it when available.
  3. 🧭AskA Bayesian policy picks the single most informative next question and stops as soon as the case is clear.
  4. 🕸MapA Legal Case Graph links people, events, evidence, legal requirements and issues — and flags contradictions.
  5. ⚖GroundSections are chosen by issue and incident date (IPC before 1 July 2024, BNS after) and shown with their verbatim text from India Code PDFs.
  6. ✍DraftA police complaint, bank complaint, consumer complaint, legal notice or legal-aid application — every fact traceable to the user’s own words.

What makes it different

🔗

Every line is traceable

Drafts are built only from facts the user stated. Tap any highlighted line to see the exact words and message it came from. Unknown details stay blanks — nothing is invented.

📜

No hallucinated law

Law text is never written by a model. 2,817 sections are copied word-for-word from 18 official bare-act PDFs, each with its source page. If a section is missing, the app says so.

📅

Old law or new law, automatically

The incident date decides between IPC / CrPC / Evidence Act and BNS / BNSS / BSA — the switch most people (and many tools) get wrong.

🆘

Safety first

Danger signals (violence, threats, an imminent land sale, fraud in progress) jump the queue with helplines 112, 181, 1930 and 15100.

🗣

Built for how India speaks

Kannada and Devanagari script, romanised Kanglish / Hinglish, speech-recognition noise, and negation like “sign maadilla” or “नहीं किया”.

🔒

Private by design

Case notes are encrypted at rest (Fernet). Accounts are optional; passwords use scrypt; e-mails are stored as keyed hashes. One click deletes everything.

LexVaani-IN, our dataset

  • 4,344 narratives across 25 legal issues in 5 domains (property, family, consumer, criminal, cyber / finance)
  • 6 language variants: Kannada, English, Hindi, Kanglish (Latin and mixed script), Hinglish
  • 2,003 speech-recognition-noise versions; 4,344 lawyer-style dialogues
  • 7,224 provision explanations — 93.5% now carry the official text
  • Contrastive, code-mixed, high-risk and out-of-scope test subsets

Under the hood

  • FastAPI · SQLite · Fernet encryption · strict Content-Security-Policy
  • scikit-learn NLU trained on LexVaani-IN (works fully offline)
  • Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for speech-to-text, follow-up answers and simpler explanations (optional)
  • Vanilla JS front-end, no trackers, no external scripts or fonts
  • Official law corpus parsed from India Code PDFs, with a verification checklist
